# Capability notes / 能力记录
**Not a product catalogue or a claim that AI can do everything.**

Only add a capability when its provider, scope, real proof, limitations and *permission for future reuse* are known. Self-description alone stays **unverified**.

| Candidate capability | Evidence level | What is **not** established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI-assisted open-source bug reproduction, native tests and controlled before/after comparisons | Demonstrated in the initiating account's separate public technical contributions, e.g. [Agent Framework issue #9561](https://github.com/microsoft/agent-framework/issues/9561), [OpenHands issue #18981](https://github.com/OpenHands/OpenHands/issues/18981), [pydantic-settings #1112](https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-settings/pull/1112) | No proof these are multi-party Co-Do projects; not a general guarantee of quality or upstream adoption |
| AI-assisted organization research and project clarification | **Proposed / partly practiced**, no Co-Do partner acceptance | No validated cross-party coordination improvement yet |
| ARIA teaming, scientific research organizing and capability exchange | **Proposed** | No qualified team or funding, no verified autonomous organization |

**Future entry pattern (use only after real work):** capability → provider → concrete use → reproducible evidence → limitations → cost → permission to reuse → next independently verified use.

**Key test:** a second, distinct project must actually reuse an authorized capability with observable benefit before claiming recursive accumulation.
