# 01 · From Tasks to Organizations
### 从任务到组织：共同研究人机网络怎样按需生成、共同生产并积累能力

**A challenging question:** If a meaningful project can assemble the capabilities and collaborators it needs *on demand*, under what conditions does that become a better form of organizing rather than just a person delegating tasks to AI?

This is also the seed of a possible working paper: **From Tasks to Organizations: On-Demand Formation and Capability Recursion in Human–AI Networks** / 《从任务到组织：人机网络中的按需生成与能力递归》.

The research itself can become a Co-Do experiment: independent researchers can change the question, introduce a counterexample, design a fair test and decide how far they want to work together.

## Three concrete research opportunities / 三个具体研究切口

### [ORG-01 · What counts as real co-production? / 怎样识别真正的共同生产？](https://github.com/flyoung588/recursive-productive-capacity/issues/5)

**What is already known:** [Flash Organizations (2017)](https://iriss.stanford.edu/publications/flash-organizations-crowdsourcing-complex-work-structuring-crowds-organizations) demonstrated dynamically assembled expert organizations; [Xiao et al. (CHI 2026)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790984) found that individual GenAI use can remain disconnected from group workflows.

**The research gap we propose:** A robust **observable distinction** between *one person plus AI doing delegated tasks* and *several independently deciding participants jointly shaping the goal and outcome*. More chat messages or contributors is not sufficient.

**Something real to co-design:** An event-level coding scheme on consented/public project histories, blind independent coding, counterexamples and inter-rater disagreement. **[Open the research problem #5](https://github.com/flyoung588/recursive-productive-capacity/issues/5)**.

*Potential perspectives:* CSCW, HCI, organizational theory, qualitative empirical methods.

### [ORG-02 · Does an on-demand team outperform stronger baselines? / 动态协作有净增量吗？](https://github.com/flyoung588/recursive-productive-capacity/issues/6)

**What is already known:** Flash Organizations demonstrate feasibility, not that every task benefits from dynamic assembly. [Pappu et al. (ICML 2026)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/pappu26a.html) observed that self-organizing LLM teams can suppress their strongest expert.

**The research gap:** In **mixed human + AI teams**, when do independently assembled collaborators achieve a better verified outcome after accounting for coordination costs—and when do they make things worse?

**Something real to co-design:** A matched task and clear criterion comparing **one capable person + AI**, **an existing small team + AI**, and **an on-demand group + AI**, measuring result quality, total effort, failure and expertise utilization. **[Open the research problem #6](https://github.com/flyoung588/recursive-productive-capacity/issues/6)**.

*Potential perspectives:* experimental design, organizational economics, multi-agent evaluation, causal inference.

### [ORG-03 · Can one project create capacity for the next? / 能力可以跨项目递归吗？](https://github.com/flyoung588/recursive-productive-capacity/issues/7)

**What is already known:** Temporary teams and artifact reuse have extensive prior art. The additional question is not whether one can save a Markdown file; it is whether a **capability produced through a real collaboration** changes a *separate* later project.

**The research gap:** Define and test a **cross-project capability transfer** that survives the original team, with provenance, appropriate rights, and an actual measurable benefit compared with ordinary documentation reuse.

**Something real to co-design:** Project A creates a method/tool; Project B, initiated independently, attempts a different task **with vs. without** the authorized capability. Track use, quality, time and coordination overhead. **[Open the research problem #7](https://github.com/flyoung588/recursive-productive-capacity/issues/7)**.

*Potential perspectives:* organizational learning, knowledge economics, reproducibility and experimental design.

## A paper that is also a research experiment

The paper is not a manifesto or a recruitment brochure. Its possible contribution is to distinguish **existing dynamic-team mechanisms** from a specific, falsifiable extension in human–AI work. An eventual working paper should report strong alternatives and negative evidence.

**What might be the first shared step?** Pick one of the issues above and propose a better counterexample, measurement method or small comparison. We can then jointly decide whether a research project—and a paper—should take shape.
