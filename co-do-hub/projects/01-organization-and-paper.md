# 01 · 从任务到组织
### From Tasks to Organizations · A research collaboration in the making

**如果组织可以像能力一样，围绕真实目标按需生成，我们应该如何理解组织？**

For a long time, production has usually started with a standing firm, institution or research group. An increasingly capable AI can help frame goals, discover missing expertise, execute work and coordinate interactions. This raises a deeper question: **Can the organization itself become an outcome of the work we choose to pursue?**

共做想让这个研究问题走出纸面：**论文的研究过程本身，就是可以共同检验的组织实验。**

## A possible working paper

**From Tasks to Organizations: On-Demand Formation and Capability Recursion in Human–AI Networks**

《从任务到组织：人机网络中的按需生成与能力递归》

The title is a starting point, not a conclusion. The paper's scope, method and eventual timing should emerge from substantive research and the people who genuinely contribute to it.

## The questions worth pursuing

1. **Formation:** What makes a group of independent people and AI agents an organization rather than just a sequence of delegated tasks?
2. **Autonomy:** When AI coordinates work, how do human participants retain the power to disagree, redirect the research and change the outcome?
3. **Capability recursion:** What can persist after a project ends? Can a new collaboration reuse methods, tools, relationships or institutions produced by the previous one?
4. **Economics:** How are real costs, value, rights and continued productive capacity handled without assuming a permanent firm?
5. **Counterexamples:** When would an ordinary research team—or one person with an AI assistant—do better?

We want a **falsifiable and practically observable account**, not a manifesto that declares itself correct.

## How the paper could itself become Co-Do

A researcher introduces a conceptual framework. Another challenges its assumptions. An AI agent maps precedents or builds a small reproducible experiment. A practitioner brings a real organizational case. The original research question changes because the collaborators have independent agency.

If a useful paper eventually emerges, the way its contributors organized and worked together becomes a second research object.

## An invitation

If your work touches organizational economics, institutional theory, collective intelligence, computational social science, AI agents or practical human–AI collaboration, **what is the most serious objection, precedent or test we should consider first?**

There is no requirement to agree with the premise. A well-grounded disagreement is a particularly valuable way to begin.
