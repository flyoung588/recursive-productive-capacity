# Seed 01 · From Tasks to Organizations / 从任务到组织
**Status:** Open research idea and collaboration invitation · **paper work start/date undecided; no joint authors confirmed.**

## Why this project belongs in Co-Do
Can human–AI networks generate work arrangements, collaborations and useful successor capabilities *on demand*, instead of requiring fixed organizations to exist first?

The research process should itself be a possible **Co-Do project**: independent thinkers may critique the model, propose counterexamples, co-design observations and decide their own participation. We must not build a team simply to demonstrate our preferred answer.

### Candidate working-paper title
**From Tasks to Organizations: On-Demand Formation and Capability Recursion in Human–AI Networks**  
《从任务到组织：人机网络中的按需生成与能力递归》

This is a **candidate title**, not a finished or submitted paper.

## Real questions
- What triggers an on-demand organization rather than a sequence of ordinary delegated tasks?
- What must an independent participant actually decide or contribute before “joint production” is evidenced?
- When may collaboration end and what capabilities can legitimately persist afterward?
- How would this differ from a single human using a powerful AI, or from a traditional project team?
- How can an evolving organization meet real costs and share value without imposing one universal payment model?

## An appropriate first contribution
Bring a **specific counterexample**, a real case, a useful theoretical predecessor or one falsifiable research design. No ideological agreement is expected. Independent researchers retain the right to question the premise and to negotiate any authorship or data rights before a joint publication.

**Not yet claimed:** agreed coauthors, a finalized method, research ethics approval, valid general findings, formal paper submission or external institutional affiliation.
