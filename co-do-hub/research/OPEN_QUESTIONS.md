# Research together · 9 specific open questions / 九项可共做科研问题

**A common research question is a better place to meet than a generic call to join a project.** Each link below opens a real research brief: what earlier studies show, a specific remaining question, a possible first experiment, and the scientific perspectives that could change the work.

这些不是“帮共做完成任务”的外包清单，也不是声称我们已发现学术界从未研究过的漏洞。每项都基于实际公开研究或官方研究需求，欢迎参与者带来强基线、反例、不同假设和更合适的研究路径。

## How to begin

Choose the **question you really care about**, not the project where your CV happens to fit. In the linked Issue, you can:
- propose an existing result that invalidates the proposed “gap”;
- specify a tighter question, data source, or better null hypothesis;
- suggest a meaningful first jointly designed experiment;
- challenge the strongest baseline, or propose an independent scientific perspective.
